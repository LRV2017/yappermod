YapperMod - plugins, themes and tweaks for Yapper
=================================================

1. Close Yapper completely (right-click its tray icon and quit).
2. Run YapperMod-Setup.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info -> Run anyway.
   The app is not code-signed, but it is safe to share.
3. Click "Install YapperMod".
4. Launch Yapper and press Ctrl+Shift+Y (or click the round Y button).

You can run the same EXE any time to update, repair or uninstall YapperMod.
After a Yapper update, run it again and click Repair.

Everything stays on your PC in %APPDATA%\Yapper\YapperMod
